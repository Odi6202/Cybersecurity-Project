Executive Summary

Cybersecurity is one of the most important sectors within the Information Technology industry because it protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital information. As businesses, governments, and individuals become increasingly dependent on technology, cybersecurity professionals play a critical role in defending systems from cyberattacks, data breaches, ransomware attacks, and other digital threats.

From a sustainability perspective, cybersecurity remains a highly valuable industry because virtually every organization relies on secure digital infrastructure. Businesses continue investing in security technologies and skilled professionals to protect customer information and maintain operational continuity. The increasing number of cyber threats makes cybersecurity an essential investment rather than an optional expense.

The cybersecurity field also provides long-term career opportunities for students and professionals. Positions such as Security Analyst, Penetration Tester, Cybersecurity Engineer, and Digital Forensics Investigator remain in high demand. At the same time, organizations face challenges such as workforce shortages, employee burnout, and the environmental impact of operating large-scale computing infrastructures.

This analysis examines cybersecurity through the three pillars of sustainability: Economic, Social, and Environmental. By evaluating these areas individually, we can determine whether cybersecurity remains a sustainable industry for future organizations, professionals, and society as a whole.
